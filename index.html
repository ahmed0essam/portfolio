Approach

Diagnose, prioritise, ship, measure

Most SEO work fails at step two. A list of two hundred issues isn’t a plan.

  1. 01

    Crawl the real thing

    Rendered DOM, not static HTML. JavaScript sites lie to crawlers, and a redirect that was fixed last month still shows as live in stale data. I verify status codes against the current response.

  2. 02

    Rank by business impact

    Every finding gets sized against the traffic and revenue it touches. Ten fixes that matter beat two hundred that don’t, and you get told which is which.

  3. 03

    Ship the fixes

    I work in the CMS where that’s the fastest route, and write implementation-ready specs where it isn’t. Framer, Webflow, OpenCart, WordPress, custom builds.

  4. 04

    Measure honestly

    If something didn’t work, the report says so. Dashboards pull from Search Console and GA4 directly, so the numbers are checkable rather than curated.
